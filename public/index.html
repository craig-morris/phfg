United States of America

PHFA Grant Programme

Funding for recently purchased homes and recently closed homeowners across the United States.

Funding Range$100,000 – $2,000,000
Grant Year2026
Our Mission A home is more than a building.

We connect recently closed homeowners with the financial resources they need to stay in their homes, reduce their debt, and build long-term stability. Whether you are recovering from a difficult purchase, consolidating high-interest loans, or planning needed repairs, our programme is designed to meet you where you are — with transparent eligibility, a real funding range, and a decision you can act on.

Eligibility Rules
  • Age above 30 years
  • Recently closed homeowners — recently purchased a home
  • Permanent / currently living in the United States
  • Good employment status
  • Retirement or veteran
Program Aims
  • Economical stimulus
  • Debt consolidation
  • Home repairs and development
  • Home purchase
  • Home downpayment
