Welcome

Welcome to the PA Housing Fund Proposal Submissions site for your organization. This portal provides program details, award information, and reporting for the Housing-Related Programs and Services program.

To begin the reporting or submission process, please review the information on the right and select the appropriate option from the dropdown. Each organization uses one login to manage all activity.

Important submission note: To prevent any potential submission errors, please only work on one application at a time. Completing multiple proposals simultaneously in different tabs/windows could overwrite other work being completed at the same time and cause a submission error.
